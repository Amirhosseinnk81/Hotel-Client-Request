================================================

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Build a scalable hotel guest service and internal operation management platform.

The system allows:

- Guests to login using personal information.
- Guests to create service requests.
- AI to analyze and route requests.
- Hotel departments to manage tickets.
- Operators to process requests.
- Managers to analyze operational performance.
